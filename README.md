# A deckbuilder

Just another Yugioh! deckbuilder

## Starting it

- **Windows:** double-click `Run.bat`.
- **Mac / Linux:** double-click `start.command` (needs Python 3).

The app opens in your browser. Keep the small window open while you use it; close it to stop.

If Windows warns you about the file, right-click the zip, choose *Properties*, tick
*Unblock*, and unzip again.

## Your decks

Decks are saved in your browser on this computer. 
To move them to another device, use *Format → Back up everything*, then
*Restore backup* there.

## Card pictures (optional)

*Format → Card images → Image folder*, then choose a folder of card images named by
passcode.

## Decklist

In the *Build* tab, switch to *Sheet*, fill in your details and click *Export PDF* to get
Konami's deck registration sheet filled in.
